Kavach Ad Blocker - Privacy Policy

Zero data collection. Kavach Ad Blocker does not collect, store on any server, sell, or share your browsing history, the pages you visit, or any personal information. It has no analytics, no telemetry and no accounts.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, paused sites, allowed sites, custom filters and zapped elements, saved in Chrome's local extension storage.
  • Blocked-request counters (numbers only, no web addresses).
  • If you turn on Sync, your settings (not your browsing) are stored in your own Google account through Chrome Sync so they appear on your other computers. You can turn it off at any time.

Network requests the extension makes

  • When you add a filter list, and every 4 days to keep it current, the extension downloads that list from the address you chose. You can turn automatic updates off.
  • The "Report broken site" button opens a pre-filled GitHub issue page in a new tab. It contains only the site name, extension version, your enabled settings and browser version. Nothing is sent unless you submit it yourself.

Why Chrome shows permission warnings

  • Read and change data on all websites: needed to block ads, hide ad elements, skip video ads and stop pop-ups on every site.
  • Read your browsing history: required by Chrome for the blocked-request counter and for closing ad pop-up tabs. This information is processed only in memory and is never saved or sent.

Acceptable ads

Kavach does not take money from advertisers and has no "acceptable ads" program. All ads are blocked unless you choose to allow them on a site.
